All figures on this page are simulated to show the target state described in the SASREF Smart Incident Management proposal; they are not SASREF records. Closure timelines: formal investigation 45 days (from the proposal); informal investigation 30, near miss 30 and safety observation 14 days are placeholders to be confirmed in discovery. Functional locations and positions are illustrative. Patterns & prediction runs on the same simulated records, with some warning sequences deliberately included to demonstrate detection; real accuracy is established only by back-testing on SASREF history. Event categories follow the SASREF incident form; mapping near misses and observations to a category is an assumption for this demo. The assistant's procedures and evidence fields are samples, not SASREF documents.
